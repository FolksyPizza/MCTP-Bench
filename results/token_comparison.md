# Token counts under multiple tokenizers

Per-scenario token counts of the full delivered content (context plus all retrievable
artifact source) under each tokenizer. Generated by `python3 run.py --compare-tokenizers`
(real tokenizers require `.venv/bin/python`).

| payment_idempotency | mctp | 754 | 911 | 769 | 754 |

Notes:
- The chars/4 heuristic is close to the modern OpenAI encodings (cl100k_base, o200k_base) for
  prose and configuration text; the older gpt2 encoding counts consistently higher.
- The direction of every flat-versus-mctp comparison is the same under all four tokenizers, so
  the qualitative results are not an artifact of the token estimate.
- Open-model tokenizers (Hugging Face `hf:<model>` for Qwen, Llama, etc.) are supported by
  `mctpbench/tokenizers.py` but were not available in this environment; adding them is future
  work.
